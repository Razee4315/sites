- Himalayan brown bear (STAR) | *Ursus arctos isabellinus* | "Bhoura Reech" (Urdu; CKNP) | Species LC globally; Critically Endangered on Pakistan's national list | Park created 1993 to protect it. ## 6. Butterflies (28 taxa, all from S2007; recorded at 13,500–14,000 ft unless stated; IUCN not evaluated unless stated)

- *Agriades jaloka* (STAR) | "its center of abundance is the Deosai Plateau"; the most common butterfly on every visit; strictly limited to the plateau.
- *Boloria sipora nitida* (STAR) | Fritillary subspecies described from Deosai; common; does not extend below the plateau.
- *Parnassius epaphus cachemiriensis* (common red Apollo) | Most common Apollo on Deosai, at 14,000 ft.
- *Parnassius delphius workmani* | Rare on the plateau, commoner at 15,000 ft above it.
- *Parnassius simo ganymedes* | Scree beside glaciers above the plateau, 15,000 ft and over; known only from Baltistan.
- *Parnassius charltonius ella* (regal Apollo; species LC) | Deosai race.
- *Parnassius hardwickii* (common blue Apollo) | Found only on Deosai and above, to 15,000 ft, rarely.
- *Karanasa modesta* | Quite common; Deosai is its type locality.
- *Pyrgus cashmirensis* | As common as *A. jaloka*; flies only in sunshine.
- *Hesperia comma shandura* | Uncommon but seen on every visit.
- *Fabriciana adippe* | Common on the plateau.
- *Vanessa cardui* (painted lady; LC) | Uncommon on the plateau.
- *Colias fieldii* | Occasional; also a museum specimen "Deosai plains", Aug 1887 (GBIF 3023164966).
- Also recorded: *Hyponephele pulchra*, *Hyponephele hilaris*, *Polygonia interposita*, *Aglais caschmirensis*, *Pontia callidice*, *Pontia daplidice*, *Pieris brassicae*, *Pieris canidia*, *Colias erate*, *Colias eogene*, *Albulina chrysopis*, *Polyommatus ariana*, *Plebejus sarta*, *Lycaena phlaeas*, *Lycaena kasyapa*.
