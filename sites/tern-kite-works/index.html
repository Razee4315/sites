01What we think

A kite is the shortest distance between a person and the weather.

Tern is four people and one long bench, on a headland where the wind seldom takes a day off.

Since 2009 we have made kites the slow way. Bamboo split by hand. Paper dyed in the yard, in whatever weather there is. Every knot tied by someone who will fly the thing before you do.

A kite that was rushed flies like it. So we make thirty-eight a season, test each one off the cliff, and send it out with three hundred metres of waxed linen and a note about the wind it likes.

03Anatomy

Four things and a knot.

Paper, bamboo, thread, paste. That is the whole list. The knot took us eleven years, and we will not be writing it down.

  1. A

    Sail

    Kozo washi, 28 gsm. Long fibres, so it tears like cloth and not like paper. Dyed in the yard.

  2. B

    Spar

    Bent over a candle to a bow of one in ten. The bow is the whole trick; flat kites sulk.

  3. C

    Tail

    Not decoration. A rudder, made from whatever was left over on dye day.

Move across the kite

  1. D

    Spine

    Madake bamboo, split by hand and never sawn. A saw cuts the very fibres the wind is going to ask for.

  2. E

    Bridle

    Two legs of waxed linen. Move the knot a finger's width and you have a different kite.

  3. F

    Seam

    Rice paste, a thumb, and a four-millimetre overlap. It will outlast the string.

04Workshop

Five days, most of them waiting.

01/ 04

  1. Monday

    Split

    A culm of madake is split in two, then four, then eight, and so on until it is either a spar or kindling. About one in three makes it. The stove does well out of us.

  2. Tuesday

    Dye

    Indigo wants a cold morning. The paper goes in white and comes out the colour of four o'clock in winter, then hangs on the line until the wind says it is done.

  3. Wednesday

    Paste

    Rice paste, a wide brush, and nobody talks. The paper is folded over a thread that runs the whole way round, so the edge can take a gust without taking offence.

  4. Thursday

    Balance

    Hung from a single thread. If it leans, a shaving comes off the heavy side. Then another. This is the day that decides whether it is a kite or a nice object.
