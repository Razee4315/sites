Parfums d'heures

Perfume is sold by the season. We find that far too slow. The light at six in the morning has nothing to say to the light at six at night, so we compose by the hour. Five fragrances. Wear the one the sky is wearing.

The atelier

Composed slowly,
in one room.

Every Cadran formula begins the same way: one hour, one place, a notebook. We go back to the same wall, the same field, the same cloister until the light has been written down properly.

Then the organ. Between forty and sixty materials for each fragrance, weighed by hand, adjusted a drop at a time until smelling it and standing there are the same thing.

  1. 01

    One hand

    Each formula is composed, weighed and signed by the same perfumer.

  2. 02

    Forty days

    Every batch rests in the dark for forty days before it is filtered and bottled.

  3. 03

    Glass and stone

    A refillable bottle, a travertine cap cut from offcuts. No box you would throw away.
